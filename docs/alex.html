Alexandre Cormeraie

Co-Founder & Developer — Connected Mate

AI & Innovation Manager for TGV Europe at SNCF Voyageurs, Alexandre is the 1st GenZ Digital Evangelist at SNCF. He shapes the digital journey aboard TGV Inouï, championing AI, agility, augmented reality and next-gen concepts to top management and executive committees, bridging generations.

SNCF Voyageurs Projects

Creator of the TGV INOUI Experience app on Apple Vision Pro — one of the first AR applications by a European rail operator, allowing users to explore the future TGV-M in immersion. He works on Agentic UI where travelers will interact with digital services via generative AI, and experiments with VR/AR headsets aboard trains at 320 km/h.

AI App Developer
