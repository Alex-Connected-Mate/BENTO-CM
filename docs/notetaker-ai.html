Key features

Smart recording

Record with one tap from iPhone or Apple Watch. Dynamic Island with real-time animated waveform. Automatic call detection with post-call notification.

Automatic transcription

High accuracy powered by Apple Speech engine. Multilingual support: French, English, Spanish, German, and more. Works 100% offline.

On-device AI analysis

Automatic summary, key topic extraction, next steps identified. Powered by Apple Intelligence — everything stays on your device.

AI Chat

Ask questions about your recordings. "What were the action items?" "What was said about the budget?" The AI answers based on the content.

Apple Watch

Control recording from your wrist. During a speakerphone call, the Watch records via its microphone. Automatic transfer to iPhone.

Organization & productivity

Add action items to Apple Reminders. Calendar integration. Filter by favorites, search by title or content. Quick access widget.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

1. Overview

NoteTaker AI is developed by Connected Mate (Alexandre Cormeraie). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles your data.

2. Data collection

NoteTaker AI does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

  • No account creation required
  • No cloud services used
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No cookies
  • No telemetry

3. Recordings and transcriptions

All audio recordings and transcriptions are stored exclusively on your device in local storage. They are never uploaded, synced, or shared with any server or third party. You have full control over your data and can delete recordings at any time from within the app.

4. AI analysis

AI-powered features (summaries, topic extraction, chat) are processed entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence. No audio, text, or analysis results are sent to external servers.

5. Microphone access

NoteTaker AI requires microphone access to record audio. This permission is requested through the standard iOS permission system. The microphone is only active when you explicitly start a recording.

6. Third-party services

NoteTaker AI does not integrate any third-party services, advertising frameworks, or analytics platforms. The App Store privacy label states: "No Data Collected."

7. GDPR & CCPA compliance

Since NoteTaker AI does not collect or transmit any personal data, it is compliant with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) by design. There is no data to access, delete, or port because no data is ever sent externally.

8. Children's privacy

NoteTaker AI does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for users of all ages.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
