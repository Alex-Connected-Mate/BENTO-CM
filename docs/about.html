About Connected Mate

Building private AI tools and decrypting technology — for everyone.

Our Mission

We believe AI should be private, accessible, and free. Connected Mate develops on-device AI tools that respect your privacy, and produces content that makes technology understandable for everyone.

What We Do

AI Applications

Free, private AI apps for iPhone and Mac. NoteTaker AI (call recorder & transcriber) and Better Dictate (offline voice-to-text). 100% on-device, zero data collected.

Tech Podcast

1,100+ episodes across 8 seasons. Decrypting tech, digital, data and AI in total simplicity. Available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube and more.

Community

An active beta community on WhatsApp. Direct feedback with the developer, early access to new features, and collaborative development.
