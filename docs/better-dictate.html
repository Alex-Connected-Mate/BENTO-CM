Three powerful modes

Push-to-Talk

Hold your shortcut, speak, release. Text appears instantly at your cursor position. Perfect for short messages, emails, and quick notes.

Dictate to Clipboard

Hands-free mode. Press once to start, speak freely, press to stop. Text is copied to clipboard, ready to paste wherever you want.

Live Translation

The magic feature. Select a target language and speak in your native language — Better Dictate automatically writes in the target language.

Features

100% Offline

All processing happens on your Mac. No data is sent to any server. Your voice never leaves your computer. GDPR and CCPA compliant by design.

Apple Silicon optimized

Optimized for M1, M2, M3, and M4 chips with Metal GPU acceleration. Ultra-fast transcription with minimal battery impact.

Custom dictionary

Add specialized words, proper names, and auto-corrections. Built-in profanity filter. AI-assisted word list generation.

Assistive technology

Designed for people with motor disabilities, speech impairments, or cognitive difficulties. Fully replace keyboard input with voice.

Choice of Whisper models

Tiny (~75 MB), Small (~500 MB, recommended), Medium (~1.5 GB), or Large (~3 GB). Choose the balance between speed and accuracy.

Discreet menu bar

The app sits in your menu bar. Always ready, always discreet. Launch at startup, automatically lower volume during dictation.

System requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4)
  • macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later
  • Built-in or external microphone
  • 1 to 3 GB of disk space
  • No internet connection required (except for initial model download)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

1. Overview

Better Dictate is developed by Connected Mate (Alexandre Cormeraie). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles your data.

2. Data collection

Better Dictate does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The application operates 100% offline on your Mac.

  • No account creation required
  • No cloud services used
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No cookies
  • No telemetry

3. Voice and audio data

Your voice is processed entirely on your device using the Whisper AI model. Audio is processed in real-time in memory and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere. Once transcription is complete, the audio data is immediately discarded.

4. Internet access

The only time Better Dictate connects to the internet is to download the Whisper AI model on first launch. After that, the application works entirely offline.

5. Third-party services

Better Dictate does not integrate any third-party services, advertising frameworks, or analytics platforms.

6. GDPR & CCPA compliance

Since Better Dictate does not collect any personal data, it is compliant with GDPR and CCPA by design.

7. Children's privacy

Better Dictate does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
