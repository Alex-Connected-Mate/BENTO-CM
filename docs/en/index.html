FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

What is Connected Mate?

Connected Mate is a French ecosystem founded in 2018 by Alexandre Cormeraie and PPC, combining an AI keynote practice, a podcast with more than 100 episodes, and a suite of privacy-first iOS and macOS apps (Better Dictate, Pock, Notetaker AI). Connected Mate SAS is based in Paris.

Who is Alexandre Cormeraie?

Alexandre Cormeraie is the co-founder of Connected Mate, a speaker specializing in artificial intelligence and innovation. He works with top business schools such as INSEAD and CEDEP, and has hosted the Connected Mate podcast since 2018. His LinkedIn profile is at linkedin.com/in/alex-cormeraie.

Where can I listen to the Connected Mate podcast?

The Connected Mate podcast is available for free on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Deezer, YouTube and via smartlink.ausha.co/connectedmate. More than 100 episodes are online, featuring interviews with French-speaking entrepreneurs, researchers and innovators.

What apps does Connected Mate publish?

Connected Mate publishes three apps: Better Dictate (on-device voice dictation for macOS), Pock (Touch Bar integration for MacBook Pro) and Notetaker AI (meeting transcription and note-taking assistant). All run on device, with no cloud server or data transfer.

How do I book a keynote with Connected Mate?

To invite Alexandre Cormeraie to speak on AI, innovation or digital transformation, contact Connected Mate through the contact page, by email at alex.connectedmate@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp at +33 7 82 15 04 38. We speak in France and internationally, in both French and English.

Are Connected Mate apps privacy-friendly?

Yes. All Connected Mate apps are built with a privacy-first approach: on-device processing, no data sent to third-party servers, and full GDPR compliance. It is a founding commitment of the company.
