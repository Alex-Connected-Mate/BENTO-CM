Key features

iPhone · Apple Watch
Apple Intelligence

01

Smart recording

Record with a single tap from iPhone or Apple Watch. Dynamic Island with real-time animated waveform. Automatic call detection with post-call notification.

02

Automatic transcription

High accuracy powered by the Apple Speech engine. Multilingual support: French, English, Spanish, German and many more. Works 100% offline.

03

On-device AI analysis

Automatic summary, key topic extraction, next steps identified. Powered by Apple Intelligence — everything stays on your device.

04

AI Chat

Ask questions about your recordings. "What were the action items?" "What was said about the budget?" The AI answers based on the content.

05

Apple Watch

Control recording from your wrist. During a speakerphone call, the Watch records via its microphone. Automatic transfer to iPhone.

06

Organization & productivity

Add action items to Apple Reminders. Integrate your calendar. Filter by favorites, search by title or content. Quick access widget.
