2. Description of the service

Pock is a personal wealth-tracking app. The App lets you:

  • track your accounts, investments and assets;
  • manage your recurring income and expenses;

Important: Pock is a tracking tool. The information displayed in the App is based exclusively on the data you enter manually. Pock connects to no bank account and provides no financial, tax or investment advice.
