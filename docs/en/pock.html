What is Pock?

Pock is a personal wealth tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. Unlike banking apps that connect to your accounts, Pock works entirely manually: you enter your balances, your assets, your goals. No bank connection, no data shared with third parties.

Why manual? Because true privacy means no one else has access to your financial data. Your information stays on your device, period. Pock is designed and developed in France by Alexandre Cormeraie (Connected Mate).

Key Features

01

Themes & Pockets

Organize your wealth by themes: Banking, Stocks, Crypto, Real Estate. Each theme contains pockets (sub-accounts) for fine-grained tracking of every asset.

02

Multi-currency

Support for EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, JPY, CAD and more. Automatic conversion using ECB exchange rates, updated daily. View your total wealth in your preferred currency.

03

Tax management

Built-in flat tax at 30%, customizable marginal tax rate. Three display modes: gross, net after flat tax, net after marginal rate. Always know what you truly own.

04

Income & Expenses

Track your salary, freelance income, dividends and other revenue streams. Log categorized expenses to understand where your money goes each month.

05

Financial goals

Set a target amount and a deadline. Pock shows a progress bar, percentage completed, and motivational messages to keep you on track toward your objectives.

06

Charts & Analytics

Interactive charts to visualize your wealth over time. Allocation breakdown by theme, gain/loss tracking, and trend analysis to make informed decisions.

Widgets & Apple Watch

Balance Widget

Add a widget to your Home Screen to see your total wealth at a glance. Updated automatically, always visible without opening the app.

Goal Widget

Display your financial goal progress directly on your Home Screen. See your target, current amount and percentage completed in real time.

Apple Watch

Check your total balance, cash and investments right from your wrist. Celebration animations when you hit milestones. Discretion mode to hide amounts in public.

Privacy mode

Shake your device to instantly blur all amounts. Password protection, Face ID authentication. Your financial data stays hidden from prying eyes.

Privacy

  • 100% local storage — your data never leaves your device
  • No server, no cloud, no account required
  • No tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs
  • The only network request: anonymous ECB exchange rates (no personal data sent)
  • Face ID and password protection
  • Shake to blur — instantly hide all amounts on screen

Your financial life is yours alone. Pock is built on a simple principle: if your data doesn't leave your device, it can't be compromised.
