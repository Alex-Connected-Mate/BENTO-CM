The observation

Take vibe coding in hand

Designers · UI / UX · Product

Everyone is talking about vibe coding. Few really know how to hold it in hand. This training is built for that.

It's no longer you drawing every screen. You launch agents that do it for you, and you keep the direction.

Designing with AI takes nothing away from your eye. It becomes your main lever: you decide, the agents execute, you correct. The time saved on production, you reinvest into what makes a good product: states, transitions, the detail.

This first Coding Mate training is built for designers. It's designed for any workstation and any corporate ecosystem, from the large locked-down enterprise to the small structure.

You'll never again have to give up animating that little button because "it would take too long."

  • 01

    More creative

    Things that were out of reach become possible: a working prototype, a complex interaction, real data that moves.

  • 02

    Faster

    You ship your products faster. An idea in the morning, a usable interface by the afternoon.

  • 03

    Further into the detail

    Micro-interactions, animations, empty states, edge cases. Everything you didn't do for lack of time.

  • 04

    Usable by developers

    A cleanly coded design that the developer connects to real data instead of rebuilding. Otherwise, it's pointless.

What changes

The Figma layer no longer exists.

The designer does it all.
The dev connects.

The world of "a request comes in, we make a Figma, there's feedback, then a developer picks it up and rebuilds it" is over. The designer designs and builds; the developer plugs the result into the real thing.

Yesterday5 steps · 2 roles handing off
  1. 01
    A request comes in
  2. 02
    A Figma mockup is drawn
  3. 03
    Back and forth, comments, versions
  4. 04
    A developer picks it up and rebuilds the design in code
  5. 05
    Goes live, often different from the mockup

The design gets lost between two roles. Every detail costs an iteration.

Tomorrow3 steps · the designer builds
  1. 01
    A request comes in → you write the spec
  2. 02
    Your agents build the coded interface; you iterate directly on it, without going back through Figma
  3. 03
    The developer connects: real data, backend, security, going live

What you design is what ships. The developer no longer interprets, they plug in.

The program

The four-day program

4 days recommended
Customizable

From spec to a coded design ready for production. Every day ends with a concrete deliverable.

01 Day 1 · Understand & equip yourself AI models: which ones, why, and how to equip yourself anywhere.

A full pass on the market's models, no jargon, with a single criterion: which one is best for your use.

Gemini (Google), Kimi (Moonshot AI), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic): why use one over the other, when a Chinese model outperforms an American one, and what tooling each vendor offers around it.

  • The panorama of models as of the session date, real strengths and limits.
  • Every tool available: assistants, coding agents, interface generators.
  • Equipping yourself for any ecosystem: locked-down corporate, SMB, freelance.
  • Installing and configuring your stack on your own machine.

By the end You know how to choose your models and tools, and your workstation is ready.

02 Day 2 · Specify & orchestrate From wireframe to spec, from spec to a first working SaaS.

You no longer design, you launch agents that design for you. They become your collaborators.

We start from a wireframe, turn it into a specification with Spec Drive, the Coding Mate spec-driven development tool, then put agents to work at scale. And we watch what happens when it's badly prompted, then well prompted.

  • From wireframe to spec: describing a UX that works, not just screens.
  • Launching several agents in parallel, splitting the work, checking what's delivered.
  • Comparing: fuzzy design specs vs. refined specs, on the same need.
  • Generating your first working app: a SaaS-style website.

By the end A working web SaaS, generated from your specs, and a method for directing agents.

03 Day 3 · Create & bring to life Designs you love: from screenshot, Figma, moodboard to code.

Your references become real interfaces. And you build a mobile app.

We test, model by model, how to go from a screenshot to something coded, then from a Figma file to something coded. You learn to direct the output down to animations and micro-interactions.

  • Screenshot-to-code and Figma-to-code: what each model reproduces faithfully, and how to fix it.
  • Your moodboard as art direction: typography, colors, motion.
  • Generating a mobile app for iOS or Android, depending on your workstation.
  • Going into the detail: transitions, states, that one animated button.

By the end A mobile app coded in your style, and a web interface refined down to the detail.

04 Day 4 · Iterate in production & connect No more Figma to make changes. And a design developers can plug in.

A Figma layer sitting on top of tools already in production: you annotate the live interface, the agent applies it.

With Annotate Kit, the Coding Mate component, you point at an element of your live web or iOS interface, describe the change, and get a precise instruction for your agent. Then we make sure everything you've built is usable by the developers behind it.

  • Iterating without Figma: annotate, correct, validate directly in production.
  • Design system, reusable components, states and accessibility.
  • What the developer connects: real data, backend, security, tests.
  • Shipping a coded design the technical team picks up without rebuilding it.

By the end A coded design ready to be connected and shipped, with the developer handover clearly documented.

The content evolves every session because the subject moves too fast. The core principles stay the same.

Tomorrow's job

Three roles, one product

PO · Designer · Tech lead

Tomorrow, digital products run by two or three people. The designer who can build keeps a central place.

The subject moves too fast to promise a fixed method. But the big shifts are clear.

In tomorrow's world, a digital product can be run by a PO and a tech lead, or by a trio of PO, designer, tech lead. A designer who knows how to build saves the PO time, and the tech lead connects it all to the real world.

The designer will need to migrate toward a PO designer role, or a tech designer role. That's exactly what this training prepares you for: staying indispensable in a tightening organization.

Role 01The PO

Owns the product, prioritizes, decides. Saves time thanks to a designer who already ships coded work.

Role 02 · YouThe Vibe Designer

Specifies, directs agents, builds the interface, iterates in production. The new PO designer.

Role 03The tech lead

Connects data, backend, security. Validates the release.

Frequently asked questions

Before you get started

Another question? Let's talk

Do I already need to know how to code?
No. Your design practice is the starting point. Your technical level is assessed during the preliminary call to adapt the path, the tools and the project. The goal is to understand what you're building and to know how to work with agents and developers, not to write code by hand.
Are the four days mandatory?
Four days are recommended to cover every topic and leave real room for practice. The training is customizable: content, pace and duration adapt to your goals. Terms are detailed in the quote.
Solo or in a group?
Both. Individually for a bespoke path, or in a group for a design team, a product team or a company. Format, location and terms are defined together during the scoping call.
What tools and subscriptions should I plan for?
They're chosen with Alexandre before the training, based on your workstation, your company's rules and the tools available. The AI subscriptions you need are provided for the training, under the terms agreed during the call.
Will my app be ready to publish?
You leave with a web SaaS and a mobile app, coded and designed to be picked up by developers. Going live remains a technical step: real data, backend, security, tests. That's exactly what day 4 prepares you for, so your developer connects instead of rebuilding.
Can I meet companies or startups?
Yes, on request. Depending on your goal and availability, Alexandre can arrange meetings with companies or startups already working this way.
What's the price, and how do I book?
The training is offered on a custom quote basis. Write to Alexandre on WhatsApp (or by email): a first conversation clarifies your needs, the format and the timeline, then you receive a tailored proposal.
