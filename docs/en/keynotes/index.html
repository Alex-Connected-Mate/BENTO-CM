Who speaks

Two speakers, two angles.

Alexandre Cormeraie

Alexandre Cormeraie, co-founder of Connected Mate, is Head of AI & Innovation at TGV Europe, SNCF Voyageurs. There he led the first voice-based TGV booking with OpenAI, the first speech-to-speech AI for agent support, the first railway app on Apple Vision Pro, and he won the Spark Spin Off prize at the France Corporate Innovation Awards 2026. He has been a faculty speaker at CEDEP since 2023, and speaks at INSEAD in executive programmes, notably for Nexus Automotive International. On stage, he talks about what he does: AI applied to real products, in production, privacy-first apps, entrepreneurship, and what happens to digital jobs when non-technical people start creating software. His conviction fits in one line: technology in the service of people, accessible and respectful of privacy.

Pierre-Philippe Cormeraie

PPC, Pierre-Philippe Cormeraie, co-founder, has been Chief Digital Evangelist of Groupe BPCE since 2017, after serving as its director of innovation. He is the author of « Réinventez votre entreprise à l'ère de l'IA », published in 2025 with a foreword by Serge Papin, and editor-in-chief of the weekly newsletter « Up to date », devoted to generative AI and followed by more than ten thousand decision-makers. A recognized speaker, he addresses digital leadership, innovation and technological disruption, in France and internationally, for executives and managers. He also hosts events as master of ceremonies.

Our themes

Five themes, which we adapt to your audience and your format. They overlap: a talk often blends two of them.

01

Artificial intelligence

AI in the service of people. Concrete applications of generative AI, shown rather than described, what it really changes in teams and products, and the questions of ethics and responsible AI that come with it. Depending on the audience, we go all the way down to vibe creation: non-technical people creating software without seeing a line of code, and the organization that requires.

02

Digital transformation

Guiding organizations through their digital shift: digital culture, change management, and what it takes for a transformation to hold beyond the plan.

03

Innovation and entrepreneurship

From idea to product. The startup ecosystem, lean and agile methods, and what separates an innovation that ships from one that stays on the slides.

04

Privacy and tech for good

Apps that respect privacy, ethical tech, and GDPR seen as an opportunity rather than a constraint. We build apps ourselves that collect nothing; we speak about it from practice.

05

The future of work

Hybrid work, AI and productivity, new ways of collaborating, and what happens to jobs when agents join the team.
