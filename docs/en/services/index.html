Under your brand

The principle is simple. We have built tools that run, we dress them in your colors, we connect them to your data, and they become your product. No "powered by" diluting your brand: you keep the relationship with your users, internal or external.

The business model is just as readable. A one-time integration fee, then a flat monthly maintenance fee: model updates, monitoring, support, evolutions. You no longer have to deal with it, and the total cost stays far below custom development.

These services are for SMEs, mid-caps and large accounts that want to industrialize AI, for communication, marketing, HR and innovation departments, for training providers, and for any organization that wants to stay sovereign over its data.

01

Creative Studio

A custom image-generation workshop, plugged into the best models of the moment and calibrated to your visual identity. Your teams produce as many visuals as they need, within your brand guidelines, without spending days on it.

  • Selected models. The generation tools closest to your art direction.
  • Controlled cost. Negotiated rates, efficient models, no waste.
  • Unlimited volume. As many visuals as you need.
  • Guidelines respected. Style, colors, typography: everything is calibrated.
02

Your company GPT

An assistant that finally answers the real needs of your people. No endless prompts, no blank-page syndrome. A simple tool, connected to your internal procedures and your data, that brings AI to everyone, not only to your power users.

  • Connected to your procedures. Augmented search over your documentation, your processes, your intranet.
  • Zero complex prompting. Guided interface, contexts preconfigured by role.
  • For every level. From the intern to the executive committee, with no prior training.
  • Fast setup. SSO, European hosting, one-time integration fee and monthly maintenance.
03

Alto

Alto is an AI-driven conversational coaching service. Your users talk with an agent that helps them clarify, structure and reach their goals, personal or professional. It deploys under your brand, whether you are a training provider, an HR consultancy, a support network or a school.

How we work

Four steps, two to six weeks depending on the service.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    A one-hour workshop, free of charge, to understand your needs, your branding, your GDPR, IT and integration constraints. We qualify the scope and price it.

  2. 02

    Branding and integration

    We dress the tool in your colors, connect your data sources, configure access. Delivery of a staging environment.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Release to production, training of internal champions, communication kit. Your teams adopt it within days.

  4. 04

    Maintenance

    Flat monthly fee: model updates, monitoring, support, evolutions, delivered to you by our R&D.
