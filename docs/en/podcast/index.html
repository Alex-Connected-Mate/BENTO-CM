The Connected Mate podcast

Connected Mate started here, in 2018: a microphone, guests, and the urge to understand what is really being built. More than a hundred episodes later, the line has not changed.

Contents

  • What it is

What it is

The Connected Mate podcast is a series of conversations with the brightest minds in tech, innovation and business. Not a news format, not a weekly digest: one person, one subject, as long as it takes to get to the bottom of it. More than a hundred episodes since 2018, hosted by PPC and Alexandre.

The podcast exists in audio, and in video on YouTube. It is where Connected Mate began, before the training, the services and the apps, and it remains the thread that ties everything else together: much of what we say on stage was a conversation at the microphone first.

The subjects follow what is moving: artificial intelligence and what it changes in companies, innovation and entrepreneurship, digital transformation, privacy, the future of work. The same themes as our talks, but the other way round: here, the guests do the talking.

Where to listen

The podcast is available on every platform.

  • Spotify
  • Apple Podcasts
  • Deezer
  • YouTube, for the video version
  • Every episode on Ausha

Subscribe on the platform of your choice to receive new episodes. The Ausha page gathers the entire catalogue, if you are looking for a specific episode or want to start again from the beginning.

If you like the podcast, you will probably like its extensions: PPC's weekly newsletter « Up to date », devoted to generative AI, and his book « Réinventez votre entreprise à l'ère de l'IA ». And if you prefer it live, our talks pick up the same threads in front of an audience.

Our guests

We invite people from tech, innovation and business who have something to tell: a product they built, a transformation they led, a conviction they tested in the field. The rest is a conversation, not a promotional interview: no pitch, no questions sent in advance to be recited.
