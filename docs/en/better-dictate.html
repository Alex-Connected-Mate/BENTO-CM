Features

100% Offline

All processing happens on your Mac. No data is sent to any server. Your voice never leaves your computer. GDPR and CCPA compliant by design.

Apple Silicon optimized

Optimized for M1, M2, M3 and M4 chips with Metal GPU acceleration. Ultra-fast transcription with minimal battery impact.

Custom dictionary

Add specialized words, proper nouns and auto-corrections. Built-in profanity filter. AI-assisted generation for your word lists.

Assistive technology

Designed for people with motor disabilities, speech disorders or cognitive difficulties. Fully replace keyboard input with voice.

Choice of Whisper models

Tiny (~75 MB), Small (~500 MB, recommended), Medium (~1.5 GB) or Large (~3 GB). Choose the balance between speed and accuracy to suit your needs.

Discreet menu bar

The app sits in your menu bar. Always ready, out of sight. Launch at startup, automatically lower volume during dictation.

Supported languages

English French Spanish German Italian Portuguese Chinese Japanese Korean Russian Arabic Dutch Polish Turkish Hindi Filipino Auto-detect

System requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4)
  • macOS 13.0 (Ventura) minimum
  • Built-in or external microphone
  • 1 to 3 GB of disk space
  • No internet connection required (except for initial model download)

Privacy by design

  • 100% offline — all processing happens on your Mac
  • Zero data collection — no account, no cloud, no tracking
  • Your voice is never recorded — audio is processed in memory and immediately discarded
  • No third-party SDKs, no cookies, no telemetry
  • GDPR and CCPA compliant by design

The only time the app connects to the internet is to download the Whisper model on first launch. After that, never again.
