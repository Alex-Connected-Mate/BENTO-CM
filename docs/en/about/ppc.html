BACKGROUND

Experience

Since 2017, PPC has served as Chief Digital Evangelist at Groupe BPCE, leading external representation of the group's data and digital transformation. He also pioneered the group's Open Data initiative — the first French banking group to open public datasets. In 2025 he published « Reinventez votre entreprise a l'ere de l'IA » (foreword by Serge Papin, former CEO of Systeme U), a strategic manual for executives reinventing their company with AI. He publishes the weekly « Up to date » newsletter (10,000+ subscribers) and co-founded Connected Mate with Alexandre Cormeraie.
