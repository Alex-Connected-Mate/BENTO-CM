Who is behind Connected Mate

Connected Mate is an independent company based in Paris. Two people carry it: one builds with AI in production, the other guides organizations through their transformation. Everything we do flows from those two practices.

The company

Connected Mate was born in 2018 with the podcast. The company, Connected Mate SAS, is an independent, bootstrapped SAS based on avenue du Général Leclerc in the 14th arrondissement of Paris. It was founded by Alexandre Cormeraie and Pierre-Philippe Cormeraie, PPC.

Four activities, each feeding the others.

  • Training. Vibe Designing, four days for designers to take vibe coding in hand.
  • Services. Four AI services deployed white-label, under your brand.
  • Talks. Keynotes and sessions on AI, digital transformation, innovation, privacy and the future of work.
  • The podcast. More than a hundred episodes of conversations with the people who make tech happen.

And, alongside, apps: NoteTaker AI, Pock, Better Dictate, Dys Keyboard. Native to Apple, built through vibe creation, and all resting on the same principle.

These activities are not four separate trades. The training passes on what Alexandre practices in production. The services deploy at your place the tools we use ourselves. The talks recount what we have seen work, and the podcast goes looking for the people doing it elsewhere.

Privacy first

Privacy is not an option, it is the architecture. Our apps collect no data and send nothing to a third-party server: all the computation runs on the user's device. Apple Silicon, Apple Intelligence, Whisper running locally: the new generation of AI runs on the device, and we are betting entirely on it.

It is also what makes us credible when we talk about it on stage or deploy a service at your place: we build tools that augment people without collecting their data, and we build them for ourselves first.

Alexandre Cormeraie

Head of AI & Innovation at TGV Europe, SNCF Voyageurs. He leads the AI and innovation strategy there: the first voice-based TGV booking with OpenAI in 2023, the first speech-to-speech AI for agent support, the first railway app on Apple Vision Pro. Winner of the Spark Spin Off prize at the France Corporate Innovation Awards 2026.

Faculty speaker at CEDEP since 2023, speaker at INSEAD in executive programmes. He trains designers in Vibe Designing, and builds the Connected Mate apps through vibe creation. Volunteer firefighter in Fontainebleau since 2018.

PPC

Pierre-Philippe Cormeraie, PPC, has been Chief Digital Evangelist of Groupe BPCE, France's second-largest banking group, since 2017, after serving as its director of innovation. He supports and champions the group's data and digital transformation plan, and led its Open Data initiative, a first for a French banking group.

Author of « Réinventez votre entreprise à l'ère de l'IA », published in 2025 with a foreword by Serge Papin, and editor-in-chief of the weekly newsletter « Up to date », devoted to generative AI and followed by more than ten thousand decision-makers. Speaker and master of ceremonies, in France and internationally.
