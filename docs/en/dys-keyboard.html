Dys Keyboard

The keyboard everyone should have had from day one.

Dys is built for dys minds — dyslexia, dysorthographia, dyspraxia. But it also helps kids just starting out, seniors getting back to a keyboard, and anyone the standard ones leave behind. Bigger keys, a color code that guides the eye and eases the memory. You find your fingers. You find your words.

What Dys changes for you, day to day

Vision, typing
Autocorrect

01

Keys that breathe

Bigger, better spaced, higher contrast. You hit what you look at — the "wrong key next door" mistakes simply stop happening.

02

A color code that organizes everything

Each zone of the keyboard has its own color. Your eye follows, your memory holds, and you remember by color rather than by position. It's the heart of Dys — especially powerful for dys minds and beginners.

03

Letters that stop tricking you

Fonts picked to clearly separate b from d, p from q, m from n. Tricky characters reworked one by one. Reading and writing simply gets clearer.

04

An autocorrect that listens

It suggests, it doesn't rewrite for you. You stay in control, always. And your hard-to-spell words have no reason to ever leave your iPhone.

05

Six moods, kind to tired eyes

Six themes to pick from, including a true High Contrast (AAA level) and a deep Dark mode. Every color tested for eyes that get tired fast.

06

Zero pressure, your pace

Set the daily goal you want. Streaks reward progress, never failure. No guilt-tripping notifications — just a keyboard moving at your speed.
