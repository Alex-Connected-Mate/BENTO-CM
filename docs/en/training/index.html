Coding Mate training · 01

Vibe Designing: become a Vibe Designer

Everyone talks about vibe coding. Few really know how to hold it. This training exists for that: you no longer draw every screen, you run agents that do it for you, and you keep the direction. What you design is what ships.

Designers, take the lead

This first Coding Mate training is for designers. UI, UX, product. It is built for any workstation and any corporate environment, from the locked-down large group to the small studio.

Designing with AI takes nothing away from your eye. It becomes your main lever: you decide, the agents execute, you correct. The time you save on production goes back into what makes a good product: states, transitions, detail.

In practice, you leave with four things.

  • More creative. Things that were out of reach become possible: a working prototype, a complex interaction, real data that moves.
  • Faster. An idea in the morning, a usable interface in the afternoon.
  • Deeper into the detail. Micro-interactions, animations, empty states, edge cases. Everything you skipped for lack of time.
  • Usable by developers. A cleanly coded design that the developer connects to real data instead of rebuilding. Otherwise it is pointless.
You will never again have to give up on animating that little button because "it would take too long".

The Figma layer no longer exists

The world of "a request comes in, we make a Figma, there is feedback, then a developer picks it up and rebuilds it" is over. Yesterday, design got lost between two trades, and every detail cost an iteration. Tomorrow, the designer does everything, the developer connects.

You write the spec, your agents build the coded interface, you iterate on it without going back through Figma. The developer connects: real data, backend, security, release to production. They no longer interpret, they plug in.

Before day 1

A thirty-minute scoping call with me, before the training, so you arrive on day 1 with the right tools and a project that looks like you. That is where we check what your company allows, and choose the models and subscriptions that fit your context.

  • Your AI subscriptions. The subscriptions you need are provided for the training, under the terms agreed on the call.
  • Your workstation and your environment. Mac or PC, iPhone or Android, your company's security rules: we adapt the tooling to your reality.
  • Your moodboard. The interfaces you love, the front ends you spotted on social networks, your references. They become raw material from day 3.
  • Your goals. The product you want to build during the training, the format, solo or as a team, and the pace.

The four-day programme

From spec to production-ready coded design. Every day ends with a concrete deliverable. Four days are recommended; content, pace and length adjust to your goals.

Day 1 · Understand and tool up

A full pass over the models on the market, without jargon, with a single criterion: which one is best for your use. Gemini, Kimi, ChatGPT, Claude: why one rather than another, and when a Chinese model beats an American one.

  • The model landscape as of the day of the session, real strengths and limits.
  • All the tools: assistants, coding agents, interface generators.
  • Tooling up for any environment, locked-down corporate, SME, freelance, and installing your stack on your own machine.

By the end: you know how to choose your models and your tools, and your workstation is ready.

Day 2 · Specify and orchestrate

You no longer design, you run agents that design for you. We start from a wireframe, turn it into a specification with Spec Drive, then put agents to work at scale. And we watch what happens when it is prompted badly, then when it is prompted well.

  • From wireframe to spec: describing a UX that works, not just screens.
  • Running several agents in parallel, splitting the work, checking what gets delivered.
  • Comparing, on the same need, vague specs and refined specs.

By the end: a working web SaaS, generated from your specs, and a method for directing agents.

Day 3 · Create and bring to life

Your references become real interfaces. We test, model by model, how to go from a screenshot, then from a Figma file, to something coded, and you learn to direct the rendering down to the micro-interactions.

  • Screenshot-to-code and Figma-to-code: what each model reproduces faithfully, and how to correct it.
  • Your moodboard as art direction: typography, color, motion.
  • Generating a mobile app for iOS or Android, depending on your machine, and going into the detail: transitions, states, that animated button.

By the end: a mobile app coded in your style, and a web interface refined down to the detail.

Day 4 · Iterate in production and connect

A Figma laid over tools running in production: you annotate the live interface, the agent applies. With Annotate Kit, you point at an element of your running web or iOS interface, describe the change, and get a precise instruction for your agent. Then we make sure everything you built can be picked up by developers.

  • Iterating without Figma: annotate, correct, validate directly in production.
  • Design system, reusable components, states and accessibility.
  • What the developer connects: real data, backend, security, tests.

By the end: a coded design ready to be connected and released to production, with the developer hand-off clearly documented.

The content changes with every session because the subject moves too fast. What is taught in a given month is what runs in production that month.

The Coding Mate tools

Two in-house tools, built by Coding Mate, used in production, at the heart of the training.

Spec Drive, day 2. "Say what you want. Watch it get built right." Your idea becomes specifications, scenarios and verifiable steps. Your agents, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, work inside a frame, and every task is proven before it counts as done. A Mac app, free and open source, everything stays local.

Annotate Kit, day 4. "Point at the pixel. Ship the change." A Figma laid over your running product. You point at an element, restyle it live, annotate, and get a precise instruction for your agent, with the component and the line of code concerned. For the web and for iOS.

What you take away

  • Choosing your AI models. Which one to use, for what, and why. And knowing how to choose again once the market has moved.
  • Choosing your tools. A stack that works inside your company, with its constraints, that you install yourself.
  • Designing with agents. From spec to interface, from moodboard to code, from annotation to fix.
  • Shipping coded, production-ready designs. A web SaaS and a mobile app, usable by your developers.

Tomorrow's job

The subject moves too fast to promise a fixed method. But the broad movements are clear. Tomorrow, a digital product is carried by a PO and a tech lead, or by a trio of PO, designer and tech lead. The PO carries the product, prioritizes, decides. The tech lead connects the data, the backend, the security, and signs off the release. Between the two, the Vibe Designer specifies, directs the agents, builds the interface and iterates in production. They save the PO time, and the tech lead plugs the whole thing into the real world.

The designer will have to move toward a PO designer role, or a tech designer role. That is exactly what this training prepares you for: staying indispensable in an organization that is getting leaner.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your design practice is the starting point. Your technical level is assessed on the preliminary call to adapt the path, the tools and the project. The goal is to understand what you are building and to know how to work with agents and developers, not to write code by hand.

Are all four days required?

Four days are recommended to cover every subject and leave real room for practice. The training is customizable: content, pace and length adapt to your goals. The terms are set out in the quote.

Solo or as a team?

Both. One-to-one for a tailored path, or as a group for a design team, a product team or a company. Format, location and terms are defined together on the scoping call.

Which tools and subscriptions should I plan for?

They are chosen with me before the training, based on your workstation, your company's rules and the tools available. The AI subscriptions you need are provided for the training, under the terms agreed on the call.

Will my app be ready to publish?

You leave with a coded web SaaS and a coded mobile app, built to be picked up by developers. Release to production remains a technical step: real data, backend, security, tests. That is precisely what day 4 prepares, so that your developer connects instead of rebuilding.

Can I meet companies or startups?

Yes, on request. Depending on your goal and on availability, I can arrange meetings with companies or startups that already work this way.

What does it cost, and how do I book?

The training is offered on quote. Write to me on WhatsApp, or by e-mail: a first exchange pins down your needs, the format and the calendar, then you receive a tailored proposal.
